## 1. Anthropic: qué dicen sobre simplicidad, herramientas, contexto, guardarraíles y evaluación

### Takeaway
Anthropic repite el mismo consejo en todos sus posts (2024-2026): «do the simplest thing that works» (haz lo más simple que funcione), pocas herramientas bien descritas, instrucciones a la «altitud correcta» (ni lógica frágil codificada en el prompt ni vaguedades), el estado fuera de la ventana de contexto (sesión / notas / compactación) y evaluaciones construidas desde fallos reales con 20-50 tareas.

### Cited Findings

**«Building effective agents» (19-dic-2024)**
- Definición: «**Workflows** are systems where LLMs and tools are orchestrated through predefined code paths» (flujos con rutas de código predefinidas) frente a agentes, donde «LLMs dynamically direct their own processes and tool usage» (el LLM dirige su propio proceso). — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents)
- Bloque básico: «The basic building block of agentic systems is an LLM enhanced with augmentations such as retrieval, tools, and memory» (el «augmented LLM»: LLM + recuperación + herramientas + memoria). — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents)
- Simplicidad y frameworks: «we recommend finding the simplest solution possible, and only increasing complexity when needed»; «We suggest that developers start by using LLM APIs directly: many patterns can be implemented in a few lines of code»; «Incorrect assumptions about what's under the hood are a common source of customer error» (suposiciones erróneas sobre lo que hace el framework por debajo son una fuente habitual de errores). — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents)
- Resumen final: «Start with simple prompts, optimize them with comprehensive evaluation» (empieza con prompts simples y optimízalos con evaluación completa). — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents)
- Patrones de workflow: «Prompt chaining decomposes a task into a sequence of steps»; «Routing classifies an input and directs it to a specialized followup task» con el ejemplo explícito de «Directing different types of customer service queries» a procesos distintos; orchestrator-workers («a central LLM dynamically breaks down tasks, delegates them to worker LLMs, and synthesizes their results»); evaluator-optimizer («one LLM call generates a response while another provides evaluation and feedback in a loop»). — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents)
- Cuándo usar agentes: para problemas abiertos donde «you can't hardcode a fixed path»; aviso: «The autonomous nature of agents means higher costs, and the potential for compounding errors» (más coste y errores que se acumulan). — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents)
- Guardarraíles y pruebas: «We recommend extensive testing in sandboxed environments, along with the appropriate guardrails»; los agentes «can then pause for human feedback at checkpoints or when encountering blockers»; «it's also common to include stopping conditions (such as a maximum number of iterations) to maintain control». — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents)
- Apéndice de atención al cliente: «Customer support combines familiar chatbot interfaces with enhanced capabilities through tool integration». — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents)
- Apéndice sobre herramientas (ACI, «agent-computer interface»): «Tool definitions and specifications should be given just as much prompt engineering attention as your overall prompts»; «plan to invest just as much effort in creating good agent-computer interfaces (ACI)»; poka-yoke: «Change the arguments so that it is harder to make mistakes» (cambia los argumentos para que sea difícil equivocarse), con el ejemplo «we changed the tool to always require absolute filepaths». — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents)

**«Effective context engineering for AI agents» (29-sep-2025)**
- Definición: prompt engineering = «methods for writing and organizing LLM instructions for optimal outcomes»; context engineering = «curating and maintaining the optimal set of tokens (information) during LLM inference» (curar y mantener el conjunto óptimo de tokens). — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents)
- «Altitud correcta» del system prompt: «the Goldilocks zone between two common failure modes»; fallo 1: «engineers hardcoding complex, brittle logic in their prompts to elicit exact agentic behavior» (lógica compleja y frágil metida a mano en el prompt); fallo 2: «provide vague, high-level guidance that fails to give the LLM concrete signals» (guía vaga sin señales concretas). — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents)
- Herramientas: «curating a minimal viable set of tools for the agent» (conjunto mínimo viable de herramientas); criterio: «If a human engineer can't definitively say which tool should be used in a given situation» (si un ingeniero humano no sabe decir qué herramienta usar, el agente tampoco). — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents)
- Ejemplos en vez de reglas de caso límite: curar «a set of diverse, canonical examples that effectively portray the expected behavior of the agent»; los ejemplos son «the 'pictures' worth a thousand words». — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents)
- «Context rot»: al crecer los tokens, «the model's ability to accurately recall information from that context decreases». — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents)
- Recuperación «just-in-time»: los agentes «maintain lightweight identifiers (file paths, stored queries, web links, etc.)» y cargan datos en tiempo de ejecución con herramientas. — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents)
- Horizonte largo: compactación («taking a conversation nearing the context window limit», resumirla y abrir ventana nueva), «structured note-taking» («the agent regularly writes notes persisted to memory outside of the context window»: notas persistidas fuera de la ventana), y sub-agentes («specialized sub-agents can handle focused tasks with clean context windows»). — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents)
- Consejo cierre: «do the simplest thing that works» es «our best advice». — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents)

**«Writing effective tools for agents — with agents» (11-sep-2025)**
- «More tools don't always lead to better outcomes»; «We recommend building a few thoughtful tools targeting specific high-impact workflows» (pocas herramientas pensadas para flujos de alto impacto, no envolver cada endpoint). — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/writing-tools-for-agents)
- Consolidación: «Tools can consolidate functionality» (p. ej. una `schedule_event` que agrupa varias llamadas). Espacios de nombres: «Namespacing (grouping related tools under common prefixes) can help delineate boundaries between lots of tools». — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/writing-tools-for-agents)
- Contexto devuelto: las herramientas deben «return only high signal information back to agents» (solo información de alta señal; nombres antes que identificadores). Eficiencia: «We suggest implementing some combination of pagination, range selection, filtering, and/or truncation with sensible default parameter values». — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/writing-tools-for-agents)
- Descripciones: escribirlas como «describing your tool to a new hire on your team»; «input parameters should be unambiguously named». — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/writing-tools-for-agents)
- Evaluación de herramientas: «Start by generating lots of evaluation tasks, grounded in real world uses»; «Each evaluation prompt should be paired with a verifiable response or outcome»; «Claude is an expert at analyzing transcripts and refactoring lots of tools all at once»; usaron conjuntos de prueba apartados (held-out) para evitar sobreajuste. — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/writing-tools-for-agents)

**«Building agents with the Claude Agent SDK» (29-sep-2025)**
- Bucle: «Agents often operate in a specific feedback loop: gather context -> take action -> verify work -> repeat» (reunir contexto, actuar, verificar, repetir). — [Anthropic / claude.com](https://claude.com/blog/building-agents-with-the-claude-agent-sdk)
- «Tools are the primary building blocks of execution for your agent». Sub-agentes «only send relevant information back to the orchestrator, rather than their full context». La compactación «automatically summarizes previous messages when the context limit approaches». — [claude.com](https://claude.com/blog/building-agents-with-the-claude-agent-sdk)
- Verificación: «The best form of feedback is providing clearly defined rules for an output, then explaining which rules failed and why» (reglas claras y decir cuál falló y por qué); LLM-como-juez: «This is generally not a very robust method, and can have heavy latency tradeoffs». — [claude.com](https://claude.com/blog/building-agents-with-the-claude-agent-sdk)
- Lista «Customer support agents:» que «handle high ambiguity user requests, like customer service tickets». — [claude.com](https://claude.com/blog/building-agents-with-the-claude-agent-sdk)

**«Equipping agents for the real world with Agent Skills» (16-oct-2025)**
- Skills = «organized folders of instructions, scripts, and resources that agents can discover and load dynamically» (carpetas de instrucciones, scripts y recursos que el agente descubre y carga); sirven para «transforming general-purpose agents into specialized agents that fit your needs». — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/equipping-agents-for-the-real-world-with-agent-skills)
- «Building a skill for an agent is like putting together an onboarding guide for a new hire» (como una guía de acogida para un recién contratado); «capturing and sharing their procedural knowledge». — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/equipping-agents-for-the-real-world-with-agent-skills)
- Revelación progresiva: nivel 1 metadatos («just enough information for Claude to know when each skill should be used»), nivel 2 cuerpo de SKILL.md, nivel 3 ficheros adjuntos «which Claude can choose to navigate and discover only as needed». — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/equipping-agents-for-the-real-world-with-agent-skills)
- Cómo escribirlas: «Identify specific gaps in your agents' capabilities by running them on representative tasks» (empezar por evaluación); «When the SKILL.md file becomes unwieldy, split its content into separate files and reference them»; «discover what context Claude actually needs, instead of trying to anticipate it upfront». Seguridad: «We recommend installing skills only from trusted sources». — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/equipping-agents-for-the-real-world-with-agent-skills)

**«Demystifying evals for AI agents» (9-ene-2026)**
- Vocabulario: «A task (a.k.a problem or test case) is a single test with defined inputs and success criteria»; «Each attempt at a task is a trial»; «A grader is logic that scores some aspect of the agent's performance»; «A transcript (also called a trace or trajectory) is the complete record of a trial»; «The outcome is the final state in the environment at the end of the trial». — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents)
- «Agent evaluations typically combine three types of graders: code-based, model-based, and human». Los de código son «Brittle to valid variations that don't match expected patterns exactly»; los de modelo son «Non-deterministic» y «Requires calibration with human graders for accuracy». — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents)
- Empezar: «In reality, 20-50 simple tasks drawn from real failures is a great start» (20-50 tareas sacadas de fallos reales). — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents)
- Qué puntuar: «it's often better to grade what the agent produced, not the path it took» (el resultado, no el camino). — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents)
- pass@k vs pass^k: «pass^k measures the probability that all k trials succeed»; «This metric especially matters for customer-facing agents where users expect reliable behavior every time» (para agentes de cara al cliente importa que acierte todas las veces). — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents)
- Multi-turno: los agentes conversacionales «present a distinct challenge: the quality of the interaction itself is part of what you're evaluating»; «they often require a second LLM to simulate the user». Saturación: «Eval saturation occurs when an agent passes all of the solvable tasks, leaving no room for improvement»; «Iterate on the evaluations to improve their signal-to-noise ratio». — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents)

**«How we built our multi-agent research system» (13-jun-2025)**
- «Bad tool descriptions can send agents down completely wrong paths» (malas descripciones de herramientas llevan por caminos equivocados). — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/multi-agent-research-system)
- «Start evaluating immediately with small samples»: con unas 20 consultas ya se veían los cambios grandes. «LLM-as-judge evaluation scales when done well»: un solo juez con rúbrica fue lo más consistente. «Human evaluation catches what automation misses». — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/multi-agent-research-system)
- «Agents are stateful and errors compound»: reanudar desde el punto de fallo, no reiniciar; «rainbow deployments» para cambiar versión sin cortar sesiones; trazado completo en producción para depurar. — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/multi-agent-research-system)

**«Scaling Managed Agents: Decoupling the brain from the hands» (8-abr-2026)**
- Tres primitivas: «a session (the append-only log of everything that happened)», «a harness (the loop that calls Claude and routes Claude's tool calls to the relevant infrastructure)», «a sandbox (an execution environment where Claude can run code and edit files)». — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/managed-agents)
- La sesión actúa «serving as a context object that lives outside Claude's context window»; «context is durably stored in the session log» (el estado vive fuera de la ventana, en un log durable). «The interfaces push that context management into the harness»; «Any fetched events can also be transformed in the harness before being passed to Claude's context window». — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/managed-agents)
- Interfaces estables: «a small set of interfaces meant to outlast any particular implementation—including the ones we run today»; «We're opinionated about the shape of these interfaces, not about what runs behind them». — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/managed-agents)
- Herramientas: cada sandbox/tool/MCP se llama con `execute(name, input) → string` («makes each hand a tool»); «For custom tools, we support MCP and store OAuth tokens in a secure vault»; «The harness is never made aware of any credentials»; «the tokens are never reachable from the sandbox where Claude's generated code runs». — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/managed-agents)
- Lanzamiento: beta pública en abril de 2026 (las fuentes secundarias difieren entre 8 y 9 de abril), cabecera `managed-agents-2026-04-01`, cobro extra por sesión-hora. — [The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/with-claude-managed-agents-anthropic-wants-to-run-your-ai-agents-for-you/); [Pebblous](https://blog.pebblous.ai/blog/claude-managed-agents/en/)

**Docs: prompt caching (plataforma, leído 10-nov-2026)**
- Orden del prefijo: «Cache prefixes are created in the following order: tools, system, then messages». «Place static content (tool definitions, system instructions, context, examples) at the beginning of your prompt. Mark the end of the reusable content for caching using the cache_control parameter». «For a prompt with a varying suffix (timestamps, per-request context, the incoming message), place the breakpoint at the end of the static prefix, not on the varying block». — [Claude docs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-caching)
- Precio: lectura «0.1 times the base input tokens price»; escritura 5 min «1.25 times»; 1 h «2 times». TTL 5 min por defecto, refrescado gratis con cada uso. Mínimo cacheable por modelo (512-4.096 tokens según modelo; p. ej. 4.096 para Haiku 4.5). — [Claude docs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-caching)
- Multi-turno: «Start with [automatic caching] for multi-turn conversations»; «use an explicit breakpoint to cache your system prompt, while automatic caching handles the conversation». — [Claude docs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-caching)

**Docs: structured outputs (plataforma, leído 10-nov-2026)**
- «Structured outputs constrain Claude's responses to follow a specific schema»; dos modos: JSON outputs (`output_config.format`) y strict tool use (`strict: true`). «JSON outputs control Claude's response format (what Claude says). Strict tool use validates tool parameters (how Claude calls your functions)». — [Claude docs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/structured-outputs)
- Garantía por decodificación restringida: «Always valid», «Type safe», «Reliable: No retries needed for schema violations». Excepciones: `stop_reason: "refusal"` y `max_tokens`. — [Claude docs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/structured-outputs)
- Límites de esquema: sin recursión, sin `minimum/maximum`, sin `minLength/maxLength`, `additionalProperties` solo `false`. Caché de gramática 24 h; «The cache is invalidated if you change the JSON schema structure or, when using both structured outputs and tool use, the set of tools in your request». — [Claude docs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/structured-outputs)

### Inferences
- Para un bot de reservas: Anthropic recomendaría un «augmented LLM» con routing simple (workflow) antes que un agente autónomo; las reglas de negocio van en el prompt a «altitud media» (heurísticas y ejemplos canónicos), no como árbol de if/else en el prompt ni como lógica frágil; el estado de la reserva se guarda fuera de la ventana (sesión / notas) y se vuelve a cargar «just-in-time».
- El criterio «si un ingeniero no sabe qué herramienta usar, el modelo tampoco» es una prueba de diseño directa para revisar el catálogo de herramientas de un bot.
- El bloque «demystifying evals» da una receta aplicable: 20-50 casos de fallos reales, graders de código donde haya verdad objetiva (slots extraídos, fecha, número de personas), juez LLM calibrado contra humano para lo subjetivo, y pass^k para agentes de cara al cliente.

### Gaps
- No encontré un post de Anthropic que diga literalmente «no over-engineer» aparte de «do the simplest thing that works» y «finding the simplest solution possible».
- El post de Managed Agents no habla de multi-tenant ni de skills; la relación skills ↔ managed agents la cubren solo docs de producto que no leí.

## 2. OpenAI: «A practical guide to building agents», Agents SDK (handoffs, guardrails), demo de atención al cliente, Structured Outputs

### Takeaway
OpenAI dice: un solo agente primero (añadiendo herramientas), instrucciones sacadas de los procedimientos y artículos de ayuda existentes, un prompt base con variables en vez de muchos prompts, guardarraíles en capas (clasificadores LLM + reglas + moderación + riesgo por herramienta) y dos disparadores de intervención humana: superar un umbral de fallos y acciones de alto riesgo.

### Cited Findings

**«A practical guide to building agents» (PDF, 2025; sin fecha impresa; cita modelos o1/o3-mini y gpt-4o-mini)**
- Definición: «Agents are systems that independently accomplish tasks on your behalf». Lo que no es agente: «Applications that integrate LLMs but don't use them to control workflow execution—think simple chatbots, single-turn LLMs, or sentiment classifiers—are not agents». — [OpenAI PDF](https://cdn.openai.com/business-guides-and-resources/a-practical-guide-to-building-agents.pdf)
- Cuándo construir uno: flujos con «Complex decision-making» («for example refund approval in customer service workflows»), «Difficult-to-maintain rules» y «Heavy reliance on unstructured data»; y el aviso: «Before committing to building an agent, validate that your use case can meet these criteria clearly. Otherwise, a deterministic solution may suffice» (si no, basta con una solución determinista). — [OpenAI PDF](https://cdn.openai.com/business-guides-and-resources/a-practical-guide-to-building-agents.pdf)
- Tres componentes: «Model», «Tools», «Instructions: Explicit guidelines and guardrails defining how the agent behaves». — [OpenAI PDF](https://cdn.openai.com/business-guides-and-resources/a-practical-guide-to-building-agents.pdf)
- Modelos: «Not every task requires the smartest model— a simple retrieval or intent classification task may be handled by a smaller, faster model, while harder tasks like deciding whether to approve a refund may benefit from a more capable model»; «build your agent prototype with the most capable model for every task to establish a performance baseline. From there, try swapping in smaller models». Principios: «Set up evals to establish a performance baseline», «Focus on meeting your accuracy target with the best models available», «Optimize for cost and latency by replacing larger models with smaller ones where possible». — [OpenAI PDF](https://cdn.openai.com/business-guides-and-resources/a-practical-guide-to-building-agents.pdf)
- Herramientas: tres tipos, «Data» (consultar CRM, leer PDF), «Action» («Send emails and texts, update a CRM record, hand-off a customer service ticket to a human») y «Orchestration» (agentes como herramientas). «As the number of required tools increases, consider splitting tasks across multiple agents». — [OpenAI PDF](https://cdn.openai.com/business-guides-and-resources/a-practical-guide-to-building-agents.pdf)
- Instrucciones (best practices): «Use existing documents: When creating routines, use existing operating procedures, support scripts, or policy documents to create LLM-friendly routines. In customer service for example, routines can roughly map to individual articles in your knowledge base» (las rutinas pueden corresponder a artículos del centro de ayuda); «Prompt agents to break down tasks»; «Define clear actions: Make sure every step in your routine corresponds to a specific action or output. For example, a step might instruct the agent to ask the user for their order number or to call an API to retrieve account details. Being explicit about the action (and even the wording of a user-facing message) leaves less room for errors in interpretation»; «Capture edge cases: ... A robust routine anticipates common variations and includes instructions on how to handle them with conditional steps or branches such as an alternative step if a required piece of info is missing». — [OpenAI PDF](https://cdn.openai.com/business-guides-and-resources/a-practical-guide-to-building-agents.pdf)
- Generar instrucciones desde documentos con un prompt: «You are an expert in writing instructions for an LLM agent. Convert the following help center document into a clear set of instructions, written in a numbered list ... Ensure that there is no ambiguity, and that the instructions are written as directions for an agent». — [OpenAI PDF](https://cdn.openai.com/business-guides-and-resources/a-practical-guide-to-building-agents.pdf)
- Orquestación: «While it's tempting to immediately build a fully autonomous agent with complex architecture, customers typically achieve greater success with an incremental approach». «A single agent can handle many tasks by incrementally adding tools, keeping complexity manageable and simplifying evaluation and maintenance». «Every orchestration approach needs the concept of a 'run', typically implemented as a loop that lets agents operate until an exit condition is reached. Common exit conditions include tool calls, a certain structured output, errors, or reaching a maximum number of turns». — [OpenAI PDF](https://cdn.openai.com/business-guides-and-resources/a-practical-guide-to-building-agents.pdf)
- Plantillas de prompt: «An effective strategy for managing complexity without switching to a multi-agent framework is to use prompt templates. Rather than maintaining numerous individual prompts for distinct use cases, use a single flexible base prompt that accepts policy variables ... As new use cases arise, you can update variables rather than rewriting entire workflows» (un prompt base con variables de política en vez de muchos prompts). — [OpenAI PDF](https://cdn.openai.com/business-guides-and-resources/a-practical-guide-to-building-agents.pdf)
- Cuándo dividir en varios agentes: «Our general recommendation is to maximize a single agent's capabilities first ... so often a single agent with tools is sufficient». «When your agents fail to follow complicated instructions or consistently select incorrect tools, you may need to further divide your system». Guías: «Complex logic: When prompts contain many conditional statements (multiple if-then-else branches), and prompt templates get difficult to scale»; «Tool overload: The issue isn't solely the number of tools, but their similarity or overlap. Some implementations successfully manage more than 15 well-defined, distinct tools while others struggle with fewer than 10 overlapping tools. Use multiple agents if improving tool clarity by providing descriptive names, clear parameters, and detailed descriptions doesn't improve performance». — [OpenAI PDF](https://cdn.openai.com/business-guides-and-resources/a-practical-guide-to-building-agents.pdf)
- Multi-agente: «Manager (agents as tools)» y «Decentralized (agents handing off to agents)». Manager: «ideal for workflows where you only want one agent to control workflow execution and have access to the user» (un solo agente controla el flujo y habla con el usuario). Descentralizado: «Handoffs are a one way transfer that allow an agent to delegate to another agent»; «especially effective for scenarios like conversation triage». Ejemplo de atención al cliente con `triage_agent` y `handoffs=[technical_support_agent, sales_assistant_agent, order_management_agent]`. «Regardless of the orchestration pattern, the same principles apply: keep components flexible, composable, and driven by clear, well-structured prompts». — [OpenAI PDF](https://cdn.openai.com/business-guides-and-resources/a-practical-guide-to-building-agents.pdf)
- Guardarraíles: «Think of guardrails as a layered defense mechanism. While a single one is unlikely to provide sufficient protection, using multiple, specialized guardrails together creates more resilient agents». Tipos: «Relevance classifier», «Safety classifier» (jailbreaks / prompt injection), «PII filter», «Moderation», «Tool safeguards: Assess the risk of each tool available to your agent by assigning a rating—low, medium, or high—based on factors like read-only vs. write access, reversibility, required account permissions, and financial impact. Use these risk ratings to trigger automated actions, such as pausing for guardrail checks before executing high-risk functions or escalating to a human if needed», «Rules-based protections: Simple deterministic measures (blocklists, input length limits, regex filters)», «Output validation». Heurística: «Focus on data privacy and content safety», «Add new guardrails based on real-world edge cases and failures you encounter», «Optimize for both security and user experience». «Guardrails are a critical component of any LLM-based deployment, but should be coupled with robust authentication and authorization protocols, strict access controls, and standard software security measures». — [OpenAI PDF](https://cdn.openai.com/business-guides-and-resources/a-practical-guide-to-building-agents.pdf)
- Intervención humana: «Implementing a human intervention mechanism allows the agent to gracefully transfer control when it can't complete a task. In customer service, this means escalating the issue to a human agent». Dos disparadores: «Exceeding failure thresholds: Set limits on agent retries or actions. If the agent exceeds these limits (e.g., fails to understand customer intent after multiple attempts), escalate to human intervention»; «High-risk actions: Actions that are sensitive, irreversible, or have high stakes should trigger human oversight until confidence in the agent's reliability grows. Examples include canceling user orders, authorizing large refunds, or making payments». — [OpenAI PDF](https://cdn.openai.com/business-guides-and-resources/a-practical-guide-to-building-agents.pdf)
- Conclusión: «start with a single agent and evolving to multi-agent systems only when needed»; «Start small, validate with real users, and grow capabilities over time». — [OpenAI PDF](https://cdn.openai.com/business-guides-and-resources/a-practical-guide-to-building-agents.pdf)

**Agents SDK docs: guardrails (leído 10-nov-2026)**
- «Guardrails enable you to do checks and validations of user input and agent output». «Input guardrails run on the initial user input»; «Output guardrails run on the final agent output». «The guardrail runs concurrently with the agent's execution». «If an agent input or output fails a guardrail, the guardrail can signal this with a tripwire». — [OpenAI Agents SDK](https://openai.github.io/openai-agents-python/guardrails/)

### Inferences
- La guía de OpenAI es la más explícita sobre «rules in context»: convierte procedimientos y artículos de ayuda existentes en rutinas numeradas, con el texto exacto de los mensajes al usuario y con la rama alternativa cuando falta un dato. Para un bot de reservas eso equivale a: una rutina por tipo de petición (reservar, cambiar, cancelar, preguntar precio), cada una con pasos y acciones claras.
- «Prompt base + variables de política» es exactamente el patrón para servir varios negocios con el mismo agente (ver §5).
- La regla de división (muchos if-then-else o herramientas solapadas) es un umbral medible para decidir si un bot sigue siendo monolítico.

## 3. Google / DeepMind: whitepaper «Agents» y Agent Development Kit (ADK)

### Takeaway
Google describe el agente como modelo + herramientas + capa de orquestación, separa «Functions» (el cliente ejecuta, el desarrollador controla) de «Extensions» y «Data Stores», y en ADK insiste en evaluar trayectoria y respuesta final con casos capturados de sesiones reales, con instrucciones claras, variables `{var}` de estado en el prompt y agentes definibles por configuración YAML.

### Cited Findings
- Whitepaper «Agents» (Wiesinger, Marlow, Vuskovic; sept-2024; kaggle.com/whitepaper-agents): definición «an application that attempts to achieve a goal by observing the world and acting upon it»; componentes: modelo, herramientas y capa de orquestación (ciclo observar-razonar-actuar con ReAct, CoT, ToT); tipos de herramienta «Extensions», «Functions» (el cliente ejecuta la llamada) y «Data Stores» (RAG); aprendizaje «in-context», «retrieval-based» y «fine-tuning»; apunta a «agent chaining» y «mixture of agent experts». — [AIGL review](https://www.aigl.blog/agents.md); [ppc.land (5-ene-2025)](https://ppc.land/ai-agents-google-unveils-framework-for-next-gen-systems/) (ambas fuentes secundarias; ver Gaps)
- ADK, evaluación: «deterministic 'pass/fail' assertions are often unsuitable for evaluating agent performance» (las aserciones pasa/falla deterministas no suelen valer para agentes); «we need qualitative evaluations of both the final output and the agent's trajectory»; «Evaluating an agent's performance requires comparing its actual trajectory to an expected, or ideal, one»; «If you intend to progress beyond prototype, this is a highly recommended best practice». — [ADK docs](https://adk.dev/evaluate/)
- ADK, criterios: `tool_trajectory_avg_score` («Exact match of tool call trajectory»), `response_match_score` («ROUGE-1 similarity to reference response»), `final_response_match_v2` («This LLM-based check is more flexible than exact matching»); eficiencia: «Compare them across runs to catch a change that keeps quality flat but doubles token usage». — [ADK docs](https://adk.dev/evaluate/)
- ADK, de sesiones reales a tests: ficheros de test «serving as a form of unit testing»; evalsets «well-suited for integration tests»; «UI tools are provided to help capture relevant sessions and easily convert them into evals within your evalset» (botón «Add current session»; se pueden «Modify agent text responses to refine test scenarios»). — [ADK docs](https://adk.dev/evaluate/)
- ADK, instrucciones del LlmAgent: «Avoid ambiguity. Clearly state the desired actions and outcomes»; incluir persona, «Constraints on its behavior», formato de salida; sobre herramientas «Don't just list tools» (explicar cuándo usarlas); estado: «you can use the {var} syntax to insert dynamic values into the instruction», y la salida «will be automatically saved to the session's state dictionary under this key» (`output_key`). Salida estructurada con `output_schema`: combinarla con herramientas es «only supported by specific models» y el ejemplo comenta «Cannot use tools=[get_capital_city] effectively here». — [ADK docs](https://adk.dev/agents/llm-agents/)
- ADK, Agent Config (YAML): «The ADK Agent Config feature lets you build an ADK workflow without writing code»; permite «just about anyone to assemble and run an ADK agent»; claves `name`, `model`, `description`, `instruction`, `tools`, `sub_agents`; «Only Gemini models are currently supported». — [ADK docs](https://adk.dev/agents/config/)

### Inferences
- La idea de Google de capturar la sesión real y convertirla en caso de evaluación (ADK «Add current session») es el equivalente práctico de «tests from production».
- Agent Config muestra que Google también trata un agente como datos (YAML con instrucción, herramientas y sub-agentes), lo que respalda el enfoque «agent as configuration» del §5.

### Gaps
- No pude leer el PDF original del whitepaper (Kaggle devolvió solo el título); las citas del whitepaper proceden de dos resúmenes secundarios. La frase sobre cuándo usar Functions («developer wants more control») no está verificada literalmente.
- No encontré guía de DeepMind específica para agentes de atención al cliente.

## 4. Expertos: Hamel Husain, Shreya Shankar, Eugene Yan, Chip Huyen, Jason Liu, Simon Willison, Dex Horthy (12-factor), Harrison Chase

### Takeaway
Los expertos convergen en cinco reglas: (1) el LLM decide y el código ejecuta («The LLM decides what to do, but your code controls how it's done»); (2) el estado vive fuera del modelo y se puede inferir de un log de eventos; (3) el error analysis manual sobre trazas reales es «the most important activity in evals», con juicios binarios pasa/falla de un experto de dominio, no escalas 1-5; (4) agentes pequeños de 3-10 pasos con pocas herramientas, dentro de un sistema «mostly deterministic»; (5) una vez que el agente ha leído entrada no fiable, no puede desencadenar acciones consecuentes (Willison).

### Cited Findings

**Dex Horthy, «12-factor agents» (GitHub, 2025; sin fecha de publicación en la página, referencia a marzo-abril de 2025)**
- Intro: «most of the products out there billing themselves as "AI Agents" are not all that agentic ... they are comprised of mostly just software»; «Get to 70-80% quality bar» y después «Realize that 80% isn't good enough for most customer-facing features» (80 % no basta para funciones de cara al cliente). — [GitHub humanlayer/12-factor-agents](https://github.com/humanlayer/12-factor-agents)

### Inferences
- «Single decider»: el patrón manager de OpenAI («only want one agent to control workflow execution and have access to the user») + factor 4 de Horthy (el LLM decide, el código ejecuta) + Action-Selector de Willison apuntan a un diseño donde una sola llamada decide y el código determinista aplica.
- «State outside the model»: factor 5 (estado inferible del hilo de eventos), sesión de Managed Agents (log append-only fuera de la ventana), ADK `output_key` en session state. Un bot de reservas debería guardar los slots de la reserva en un objeto de estado persistido, no fiarse de que estén en el historial.
- «Natural-language rules vs code»: nadie recomienda meter toda la lógica en prompt; Anthropic pide «altitud correcta» con ejemplos; OpenAI pide rutinas numeradas con acciones explícitas; Horthy pone las acciones de alto riesgo y los límites en código (factor 8). Regla de corte útil: lo que es política de negocio verificable (precio, cupo, horario) va en código/datos; lo que es juicio conversacional va en prompt con ejemplos.
- «Tests from production»: Hamel (≥100 trazas, open/axial coding, primer fallo), Anthropic (20-50 tareas de fallos reales), ADK («Add current session»), Jason Liu (clusters de consultas reales). Todos construyen el banco de pruebas desde conversaciones reales y lo revisan con un experto de dominio.
- Sobre la inyección: un bot de reservas que lee mensajes de clientes (no fiables), accede a datos privados (reservas, teléfonos) y envía mensajes externos (WhatsApp/correo) tiene la trifecta completa; Willison diría que las acciones consecuentes (cobrar, cancelar, mandar correo a terceros) no pueden depender de texto que el cliente controla.

## 5. Multi-tenant / white-label: el mismo agente para varios negocios cambiando conocimiento, procedimientos y herramientas

### Takeaway
No hay un documento canónico de los laboratorios sobre white-label, pero tres piezas apuntan al mismo diseño: el «prompt base con variables de política» de OpenAI, las Agent Skills de Anthropic (procedimientos como carpetas de datos con revelación progresiva) y el Agent Config en YAML de Google; los blogs de ingeniería de plataformas multi-tenant concretan un objeto de configuración por tenant (prompt, herramientas habilitadas, ids de base de conocimiento, modelo, reglas de negocio) y aislamiento de conocimiento por namespace.

### Cited Findings
- OpenAI: «use a single flexible base prompt that accepts policy variables ... As new use cases arise, you can update variables rather than rewriting entire workflows»; ejemplo con `{{user_first_name}}`, `{{user_tenure}}`, `{{user_complaint_categories}}`. — [OpenAI PDF](https://cdn.openai.com/business-guides-and-resources/a-practical-guide-to-building-agents.pdf)
- OpenAI: «routines can roughly map to individual articles in your knowledge base» (los procedimientos salen de la base de conocimiento del negocio). — [OpenAI PDF](https://cdn.openai.com/business-guides-and-resources/a-practical-guide-to-building-agents.pdf)
- Anthropic Skills: «organized folders of instructions, scripts, and resources that agents can discover and load dynamically»; «transforming general-purpose agents into specialized agents that fit your needs»; «packaging your expertise into composable resources for Claude»; metadatos de nivel 1 para saber «when each skill should be used». — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/equipping-agents-for-the-real-world-with-agent-skills)
- Anthropic Managed Agents: herramientas uniformes vía `execute(name, input) → string`; MCP con «OAuth tokens in a secure vault»; «The harness is never made aware of any credentials» (las credenciales por cliente viven fuera del harness y del sandbox). — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/managed-agents)
- Anthropic prompt caching: el prefijo cacheable es tools → system → messages; «Place static content (tool definitions, system instructions, context, examples) at the beginning» (en multi-tenant, el bloque común va delante y el bloque del negocio detrás para compartir caché). — [Claude docs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-caching)
- Google ADK Agent Config: agente definido en YAML con `name`, `model`, `description`, `instruction`, `tools`, `sub_agents`; «lets you build an ADK workflow without writing code». — [ADK docs](https://adk.dev/agents/config/)
- OpenAI Agents SDK: estado de aplicación por ejecución en `RunContextWrapper.context`, no en el prompt. — [OpenAI Agents SDK](https://openai.github.io/openai-agents-python/handoffs/)
- Sierra (plataforma comercial de atención al cliente; página de producto): «Compose AI agents by mixing and matching skills—like triage, respond, and confirm—into complex workflows»; «Set clear objectives and guardrails to help your agent achieve its goals while adhering to policy guidelines»; «Define the degree of flexibility your agent should exhibit for each workflow»; simulaciones: «Verify your agent performs as expected across a wide range of scenarios and avoid regressions»; cliente Ramp: «write customer journeys as code». — [sierra.ai](https://sierra.ai/product/agent-sdk)
- CallSphere, «Building a multi-tenant agentic AI platform» (16-mar-2026; blog de proveedor): esquema `TenantAgentConfig` con `system_prompt_template: str`, `enabled_tools: list[str]`, `knowledge_base_ids: list[str]`, `model_name: str`, `escalation_keywords`, `business_hours`, `max_conversation_turns: int = 50`; «At runtime, assemble the agent using the tenant's configuration» («Dynamic Agent Assembly»); «Each tenant's knowledge base must be completely isolated» con «namespaces or separate collections per tenant» (`tenant_{tenant_id}`). — [CallSphere](https://callsphere.ai/blog/building-multi-tenant-agentic-ai-platform-architecture)
- Otras páginas de proveedores (marketing): deployments «multi-tenant from the ground up» con permisos y facturación por tenant; un modelo de «three-layer inheritance» con «Platform defaults» como base; branding por partner como datos estructurados y «The prompt builder ensures the agent always identifies as the partner's product, never reveals the underlying platform». — [chatbotkit](https://chatbotkit.com/solutions/whitelabel); [CallSphere white-label](https://callsphere.ai/blog/building-white-label-agentic-ai-reseller-platform)

### Inferences
- El diseño que sale de juntar las fuentes: un agente genérico (bucle, herramientas, guardarraíles) + por negocio un paquete de datos con (a) conocimiento (artículos/FAQ/precios), (b) procedimientos como rutinas o skills cargables por metadatos, (c) lista de herramientas habilitadas y credenciales en vault, (d) variables de política para el prompt base. Las reglas verificables (cupos, precios, horarios) van en datos; las de trato van en prompt.
- La revelación progresiva de las Skills de Anthropic resuelve el coste de contexto: el agente solo carga el procedimiento del negocio cuando los metadatos indican que aplica.
- El orden de caché de Anthropic sugiere colocar el bloque común a todos los negocios primero y el bloque específico después, para compartir el prefijo entre tenants.

### Gaps
- Ningún laboratorio publica una guía titulada «multi-tenant agents» o «agent as configuration»; las fuentes son indirectas (plantillas de prompt, skills, YAML) y blogs de proveedores con interés comercial. La «three-layer inheritance» de CallSphere quedó truncada en el buscador y no la pude verificar.
- No encontré guía sobre aislamiento de credenciales de herramientas por tenant más allá del vault de Managed Agents.
- Sierra no publica detalles técnicos de cómo sirve varias marcas con la misma plataforma; su página habla de canales, no de marcas.
